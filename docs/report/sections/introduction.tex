\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Problem Statement}

Hardware stores face unique operational challenges that differentiate them from other retail businesses:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Complex Inventory:} Thousands of SKUs with varying units (pieces, meters, kilograms, liters), diverse price points, and multiple suppliers per product.
    
    \item \textbf{Manual Stock Tracking:} Traditional paper-based systems or basic spreadsheets lead to stock discrepancies, outdated pricing, and inability to track inventory movements accurately.
    
    \item \textbf{Slow Checkout Process:} Without integrated barcode scanning and automated price lookup, cashiers waste time searching for products and calculating totals manually.
    
    \item \textbf{Reactive Replenishment:} Store owners discover stockouts only when customers request unavailable items, resulting in lost sales and poor customer experience.
    
    \item \textbf{Supplier Management Complexity:} Multiple suppliers for the same product, varying lead times, and manual purchase order creation increase procurement overhead.
    
    \item \textbf{Lack of Business Insights:} Without real-time analytics, owners struggle to identify best-selling products, slow-moving inventory, profit margins, and sales trends.
    
    \item \textbf{Multilingual Requirements:} In regions like Morocco, businesses need both French and Arabic interfaces to serve diverse customer bases and staff.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Solution Overview}

The Quincaillerie Application addresses these pain points through a modern, integrated web-based platform that digitizes and automates hardware store operations. The system provides:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2cm,style=nextline]
    \item[Centralized Product Database] Single source of truth for all products with support for multiple languages, barcodes, SKUs, images, categories, and supplier relationships.
    
    \item[Professional POS Interface] Barcode-optimized checkout with audio feedback, keyboard shortcuts, real-time stock validation, and instant PDF invoice generation in French or Arabic.
    
    \item[Intelligent Replenishment] Statistical demand forecasting using moving averages and exponential smoothing to generate prioritized reorder recommendations with supplier suggestions.
    
    \item[Automated Purchase Workflow] Complete purchase order lifecycle from draft creation through supplier communication to goods receipt with automatic stock updates.
    
    \item[Real-Time Analytics] Interactive dashboards displaying sales trends, inventory turnover, profit margins, category performance, and anomaly detection alerts.
    
    \item[Role-Based Access Control] Three-tier permission system (Admin, Cashier, Viewer) ensuring users access only appropriate features.
\end{description}
